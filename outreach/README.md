# Outreach Autopilot

The free sales engine from the business plan: a Google Sheet plus an Apps Script that finds a contact email for each prospect, sends your first email, follows up twice, and stops the moment someone replies, opts out or bounces. It runs inside your own Google account and costs nothing.

- `Outreach-Autopilot-template.xlsx` — the sheet (Start here, Prospects, Settings, Do not contact, Log, Dashboard). Upload it to Google Drive and save it as a Google Sheet.
- `Code.gs` — paste into the sheet's Apps Script editor (Extensions → Apps Script).
- `SETUP.txt` — the 15-minute setup, the Outreach menu, the sending rules and what to do when something goes wrong. Read it before you send anything.

The email templates live on the sheet's Settings tab; the same templates, the daily routine and the legal rules are also in the app under **Admin → Business plan**, so you can copy them from there.

Two rules that matter more than the rest: only email UK limited companies, LLPs and public bodies or US businesses (sole traders need consent first; skip the EU), and never send more than 30 emails a day from one mailbox. Start at 10 a day.
