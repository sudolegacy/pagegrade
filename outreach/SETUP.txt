PageGrade Outreach Autopilot: setup
===================================

What's in this zip
- Code.gs: the script. You paste it into the sheet's Apps Script editor.
- Outreach-Autopilot-template.xlsx: the sheet (Start here, Prospects, Settings, Do not contact, Log, Dashboard).
- SETUP.txt: this file.

It costs nothing. It runs on Google Sheets and Apps Script, inside your own Google account,
and sends from the Gmail account that owns the sheet.


Set up (about 15 minutes)
-------------------------
1.  Use a separate Gmail account for outreach, under your real name. Not the account PageGrade
    sends its own emails from, so a problem with outreach can't affect the app's emails.
2.  Upload the .xlsx file to Google Drive in that account. Open it with Google Sheets, then
    File > Save as Google Sheets.
3.  Extensions > Apps Script. Delete the sample code, paste everything from Code.gs, click Save.
4.  Recommended: in Apps Script, click + next to Services, choose Gmail API, click Add.
    Follow-ups then stay in the same email thread. Without it they arrive as a new email
    starting with "Re:".
5.  Reload the sheet. An Outreach menu appears. The first time you use it, Google asks for
    permission. Because it's your own script, Google shows a warning: click Advanced, then
    Go to the project.
6.  Fill in the yellow cells on the Settings tab (your name, company line with the registered
    name, number and where it's registered, registered office address, sample report link,
    privacy link). Add a short section to your privacy page about outreach: where
    you find business contacts (company websites and directories), why (legitimate interests),
    how long you keep them, and how to opt out. Read the email templates and change them to
    sound like you.
7.  Delete the two example rows on Prospects. Add real prospects: Company, Website, City, Niche
    and Track (Agency, Owner or Partner). For Owner rows, also paste the Fixes and the Report
    link from PageGrade's "Email the fixes" (do owner reviews in your main account).
8.  Outreach > Find emails for new rows. Check each row: right business, a UK limited company,
    LLP or public body, or a US business, and a real address. Then set OK to email to Yes.
9.  Outreach > Preview next email, then Outreach > Send a test email to me. Read it on your phone.
10. Outreach > Start autopilot.


What it does on its own
-----------------------
- Every hour during your sending hours (default 9:00 to 17:00, weekdays), it checks for
  replies, sends follow-ups that are due, then sends new emails, up to your daily limit
  (default 10 a day, follow-ups included). It spreads the day's emails across the hours.
- Follow-ups: up to 2, 4 days apart.
- It stops emailing someone as soon as they reply (row turns yellow), opt out (grey, and they
  go on the Do not contact tab) or bounce (red). Out-of-office replies are ignored. Replies that
  arrive up to 45 days after the last follow-up are still caught.
- It never emails a row unless OK to email is Yes. It never emails the same address twice, and
  only one person per company domain (free addresses such as Gmail or Outlook excepted).
- If a row is missing something its template needs (for example the report link), the row waits
  and the Script note says what to fill in. It sends on its own once you fill it in.
- It keeps a Log and a Dashboard (contacted, replies, opt-outs, bounces and rates).


The Outreach menu
-----------------
- Find emails for new rows: for each row with a website but no email, it visits the home,
  contact and about pages, fills in a contact email on the company's own domain, and notes any
  company number it sees (confirm it on Companies House). Up to 40 rows per run: run it again
  for more. It respects robots.txt. It never sets OK to email.
- Preview next email / Send a test email to me: the test goes only to you and changes nothing.
- Send next batch now: sends now, even outside sending hours or when Paused, but never over
  the daily limit.
- Check replies now.
- Start autopilot / Stop autopilot. To pause for a while, set Paused to Yes in Settings.


Rules (important)
-----------------
- Only set OK to email to Yes for UK limited companies, LLPs and public bodies, or US
  businesses. UK sole traders and most partnerships need consent first. Skip the EU and other
  countries unless you've checked their rules.
- Keep the footer: your name, company, registered office address, privacy link and the
  opt-out line.
- Never remove anyone from Do not contact. You can add a whole domain, like @example.co.uk.
- Raise the daily limit slowly: 10 a day in week 1, 20 in week 2, then up to 30. Never more
  than 30 a day from one mailbox. Keep emails plain, with one link and no attachments.
- If bounces go over 3% or opt-outs over 2%, pause and fix your lists or your emails.
- If you use more than one mailbox, give each its own copy of this sheet with a different set of
  prospects (for example UK and US), and copy new opt-outs into every copy each week.


Google's limits
---------------
- Free Gmail accounts can email about 100 people a day from scripts (Google Workspace: 1,500).
- Scripts run by the hourly trigger can use about 90 minutes a day on free accounts (6 hours on
  Workspace). A normal run takes well under a minute.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- "The Prospects tab is missing these columns": don't rename or delete the Prospects headers.
- Nothing sends: check that OK to email is Yes, Paused is No, it's inside your sending hours,
  the daily limit isn't used up, and read the row's Script note.
- Follow-ups arrive as separate emails: add the Gmail API service (step 4).
- Google emails you about a failed run: open Apps Script > Executions to see the message.
- To stop everything at once: Outreach > Stop autopilot.
